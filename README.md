# Callum Cockburn | Senior Technical Innovation Manager & Systems Engineer

Callum Cockburn is Senior Technical Innovation Manager at Synoptix, where he leads and supports research, technical strategy and the development of new capabilities and services. His work spans early-stage research through to product development and client delivery, alongside technology governance and the development of the department and its engineers. 
He develops strategic relationships across industry and academia, secures research funding and shapes collaborative research programmes. He has also helped develop Synoptix’s healthcare offering, building relationships with NHS stakeholders and exploring where systems engineering can help address healthcare challenges. 
Callum leads Synoptix’s AI assurance capability and authored its Assurance by Design framework. His work examines how evidence, engineering decisions and organisational controls support the responsible use of AI systems. He combines client delivery with contributions to national consultations and industry discussions on AI assurance and governance.
His wider technical interests include digital twins, autonomous systems and advanced modelling and simulation, particularly in safety-critical and regulated settings. He is an Incorporated Engineer and a certified AI Management Practitioner.
